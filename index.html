SOLAR SYSTEM

Visualization, not an ephemeris. Orbit positions use circular orbits with approximate periods and arbitrary start angles. Planet sizes and distances are compressed for visibility (real relative sizes are listed in the info panel). The sun's light does not fade with distance, and no external textures are used (all surfaces are procedural art).
